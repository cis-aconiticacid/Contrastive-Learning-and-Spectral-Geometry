checkouts are not duplicated here.

  tree includes local research modifications and retains the upstream license.
- `stable-pretraining`: https://github.com/rbalestr-lab/stable-pretraining at
  `0cd795470dc7998bc93f079d4bdaf0a7a2a516df`.
- `stable-worldmodel`: https://github.com/rbalestr-lab/stable-worldmodel at
  `e2b22b6a909039ffb29ae316777103d90947ac06`.

Large pretrained checkpoints and datasets should be obtained from their
original providers. Their copies in the abandoned workspace were excluded.
